Six months after the cataclysm ended
The shattered realm began its fragile recovery

To honour the hero who saved the nation
A grand statue was erected in memory of the Bravant

Peace seemed to be returning at last
And life was slowly finding its rhythm

Yet fate was not ready to show mercy

On that dark day, the spire fell from the heavens

A terrifying legion burst forth, beginning a swift and ruthless invasion

Within moments, vast lands were lost to darkness

The royal army and elite guard fought desperately to hold the palace
But against such fury, no one knew how long they could endure

Desperate, the king ordered his guard to flee with the princess
But the princess had her own plans...
